#### Overall Impression
A solid functional terminal that is currently held back by leftover decorative CSS. The single biggest opportunity is to ruthlessly strip out all shadows and layout animations to fully align with the "Flat-By-Default" rule defined in `DESIGN.md`.

#### What's Working
- **Keyboard Navigation**: The inclusion of `<KeyboardShortcuts />` at the root level is a massive win for the target "Power User" persona.
- **Color Strategy in Landing Page**: The new dark-first, emerald-accented landing page perfectly nails the Vercel/Linear vibe.
- **Routing Structure**: Clear separation between signed-in and signed-out states.

#### Priority Issues

**[P1] Leftover Soft Shadows and Side-Tabs**
- **What**: `index.css` still contains `box-shadow: var(--shadow-glass)` and `border-right: 1px solid var(--border-subtle)` for `.card` or `.drawer` elements.
- **Why it matters**: It directly violates the "Flat-By-Default" and "Surgical Precision" rules in `DESIGN.md`, making the app look like a generic AI template rather than a premium terminal.
- **Fix**: Remove all `box-shadow` properties (except the intended ambient glows) and the thick side-tab borders.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish` or `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Layout Thrashing on Progress Bars**
- **What**: `.sector-bar-fill` and `.stop-bar-fill` animate their `width` property directly.
- **Why it matters**: Animating layout properties causes paint thrashing and makes the interface feel janky on lower-end devices.
- **Fix**: Refactor progress bar fills to use `transform: scaleX(...)` with `transform-origin: left` instead of animating `width`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable animate`

**[P2] Navigation Cognitive Load**
- **What**: The main sidebar has 7 top-level links.
- **Why it matters**: 7 items is pushing the boundary of working memory (Miller's Law). Users have to scan too many items to find their tool.
- **Fix**: Group the navigation into categories (e.g., "Execution" vs "Analysis") or reduce top-level items.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User)**: 
- The `transition: width 0.6s` on the progress bars is too slow. Alex expects instant data rendering. The "Fast, purposeful, almost imperceptible" rule from DESIGN.md is being violated here.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)**: 
- 1px `#262626` borders on a `#0a0a0a` background might lack sufficient contrast to distinguish interactive cards from the background for users with low vision.

#### Minor Observations
- The `landing-container` and other old CSS classes are fully removed, but `index.css` is still quite large (2600+ lines). It might benefit from being split into component-specific modules or migrated fully to Tailwind.
