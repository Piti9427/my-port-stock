## Working Thesis

Space data centers / orbital compute are an early-stage extension of the AI infrastructure buildout. The thesis is driven by terrestrial bottlenecks:

- Grid connection delays
- Power availability
- Cooling constraints
- Land and permitting pressure
- Satellite data processing latency and downlink limits

The theme is real enough for watchlist tracking, but not mature enough to justify buying public space stocks without current catalyst proof, entry discipline, and risk/reward.
