# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Elite institutional investors, Chief Investment Officers (CIOs), and highly disciplined quantitative traders. They operate in high-stakes environments where every decision is backed by hard data, strict risk management, and multi-agent AI consensus.

## Product Purpose
To serve as an uncompromising, AI-powered trading terminal. It orchestrates a council of specialized AI sub-agents (Fundamental, Quant, Macro, Risk, Catalyst) to deliver real-time market verdicts, precise scenario planning, and post-mortem trade journaling. Success means the user acts with absolute confidence and zero emotional bias.

## Brand Personality
Elite, institutional, commanding, and terminal-native. The voice is direct, technical, and data-driven. It evokes the feeling of being inside a high-end algorithmic trading floor at midnight.

## Anti-references
- Generic "B2B SaaS" white/cream dashboards with soft shadows and rounded playful illustrations.
- Overly gamified retail trading apps (no confetti, no "to the moon" slang, no bright primary colors).
- The 2023-era "AI scaffold" (tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section).
- Cluttered, overwhelming legacy financial software that lacks clear visual hierarchy.

## Design Principles
1. **Data over Decoration:** Every pixel must earn its place by conveying market intelligence or agent status. No purely decorative elements.
2. **Confident Action:** Buttons and interactive elements must look precise and intentional. R/R (Risk/Reward) ratios and Stops are hard gates, not suggestions.
3. **Systematic Predictability:** The UI must behave like a well-oiled machine. Status indicators, colors (Profit/Loss), and terminology must remain perfectly consistent across the Dashboard, AI Floor, and Journal.
4. **Institutional Restraint:** Use motion strictly to convey agent lifecycle state (Spawned, Working, Presenting) or critical alerts, not for visual flair.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast requirements strictly enforced for dark mode (Terminal Void).
- Critical data (like price changes and verdicts) must rely on more than just color (use typography weight, +/- symbols, and icons).
- Animations on the AI Floor must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks.
