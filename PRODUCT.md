# Product


## Users
Traders and investors who rely on a systematic, quantitative Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) to make buy/sell/hold decisions. They need quick, dense, and reliable snapshots of market data.

## Product Purpose
A personal AI trading assistant (Market Oracle Dashboard) that provides real-time quantitative screening and decision snapshots based on a strict set of technical and fundamental rules.

## Brand Personality
Precise, modern, dark-first, and highly legible. It follows the Linear/Vercel aesthetic: minimal borders, high-contrast typography, and purposeful use of status colors (success, danger, warning). "ไม่เป็น AI slop".

## Anti-references
- "AI slop" or overly glowing, neon aesthetics that distract from the data.
- Generic, overly colorful SaaS dashboards.
- Over-decorated cards with unnecessary blurs (glassmorphism) unless it specifically serves the visual hierarchy.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over decoration**: Data must be the focal point.
- **Earned familiarity**: Use standard patterns for financial tables and metric displays.
- **Decisive feedback**: Status (Buy/Hold/Avoid) must be immediately scannable through strict color coding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast mode by default (dark background, light text).
- Clear, distinct colors for positive and negative financial indicators.
